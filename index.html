ABOUT

Why Netball Stats Pro Exists

Netball Stats Pro started from a simple idea: performance data should be easy to collect, easy to understand and available to every team.

The Problem

Many coaches still rely on handwritten notes, spreadsheets or expensive software that can be difficult to use during live matches. Valuable performance information often gets lost or never gets analysed properly.

Why I Stared Building It

I wanted a better way to track what really happens during a game. The goal was to create a platform that could capture live match events, track player performance and turn statistics into meaningful insights without requiring hours of manual analysis afterwards. The coach doesnt have to struggle to decide who to chose to play a final or which player will fit a cetrain position better, this app will give you insights that will make this decision way easier for the coaches.

The Vision

Netball Stats Pro is being built to help coaches, analysts and players make smarter decisions through data. By combining live tracking, performance analytics and future AI-powered insights, the platform aims to make professional level match analysis accessible to every team.

Where We're Going

This is only the beginning. Future development includes AI tactical insights, cloud synchronization, team collaboration tools and advanced reporting features designed to help teams unlock their full potential.
